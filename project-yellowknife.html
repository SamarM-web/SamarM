GW Data Science for Sustainable Development (DSSD) · Spring 2024

Yellowknife Wildfire Social Media Analysis

How did people use Facebook and Twitter during the 2023 Yellowknife wildfire evacuation? An NLP and network analysis of public posts, done with Sustainable GW.

  • 950Facebook search posts
  • 405safety-group posts
  • ~500tweets

About 20,000 residents evacuated Yellowknife in August 2023, and much of the community’s communication happened on social media. Because platform restrictions blocked standard collection, our team scraped public posts with web-scraping tools, removed poster names for privacy, and cleaned and standardized the text and timestamps for analysis.

We classified sentiment with the Twitter-roBERTa transformer model, built word clouds and sentiment time series, and ran topic modeling and keyword-based categorization into themes like government aid, community aid and dissatisfaction. Network analysis with degree and betweenness centrality showed which posters were most central to spreading safety information. Neutral, informational posts dominated, safety and evacuation keywords peaked on August 17, and community aid was the most discussed theme.
