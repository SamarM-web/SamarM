About me

Hi, I’m Samar. I’m a statistician and data scientist with a B.S. in Statistics and a B.A. in Philosophy from The George Washington University. My work centers on statistical modeling, machine learning and causal inference, and I enjoy projects that take messy, real-world data all the way to clear results people can use.

I’m currently a data scientist in GWU’s Dynamic Online Networks Lab, where I build models of collective behavior online and offline, and a researcher with Future Worlds Center in Cyprus, where I develop cost models for structured dialogue. I’ve also worked as a quality analyst at Mary’s Center in Washington, DC, and as a data analyst intern at DC Witness. I work mainly in Python, R and SQL, hold a Six Sigma White Belt, and am a member of the American Statistical Association and the Association for Women in Mathematics.

Outside of research, I tutor math and statistics, and I love to travel. All references are available upon request.
